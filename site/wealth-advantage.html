Who ultimately pays?

A tax deduction or deferral can reduce federal revenue relative to the alternative tax treatment. It does not prove an identical dollar increase on any particular family’s tax bill. To quantify the public cost, Power Atlas must pair these legal rules with Treasury/JCT tax-expenditure estimates, utilization data, and distributional analysis. Those figures are not invented here.

Deduction ≠ cash

A $100,000 qualifying deduction at a 37% marginal federal rate can reduce tax by up to $37,000 if usable—not return $100,000 in cash.

Business use ≠ personal use

Recordkeeping, business purpose, at-risk, passive-loss, basis and recapture rules can sharply reduce or disallow a claimed benefit.

Access ≠ exclusivity

Small businesses, employees with investments, homeowners and heirs can qualify for many of these rules too. Wealth may make them easier to use at scale.
