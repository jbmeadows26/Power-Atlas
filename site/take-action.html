WHY WE SET LIMITS

Keep civic action credible.

  • One topic from Power Atlas research at a time.
  • 280-character optional firsthand note. No attachments or external links.
  • No autofill or auto-send. You review and submit through the official office.
  • No platform inbox. We do not store names, full addresses, messages, or submission histories.

Congressional offices may require name, address, or residency checks on their own websites. Their privacy policies apply there.
