Story 03 · Consumer Watch

What does that phone promotion really cost?

Promotional prices rarely tell the whole story. Change the assumptions to compare an unlocked purchase with a financed promotion that requires a more expensive plan and a trade-in.

Illustrative inputs only—not a verified Verizon, T-Mobile or AT&T offer. Same taxes, fees and non-plan usage costs are omitted from both scenarios. Assumes the promotion requires the old phone as a trade-in and credits pay out in full.

Effective cost comparisonOver 36 months
Buy unlocked + sell old phone$849
Carrier promotion + trade-in$1,269
Under these assumptions, the carrier promotion costs $420 more over 36 months.

Unlocked = retail price − resale proceeds. Promo = retail price − credits + (required plan increase × months). Both surrender the old phone, by resale or trade-in. Early cancellation, lost promotional credits, taxes, fees, financing terms and eligibility rules can change the result.
