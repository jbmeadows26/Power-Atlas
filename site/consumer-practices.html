THE EVIDENCE STANDARD

No verdicts by graphic design.

What we can document

Published plan restrictions, regulator complaints, court actions, announced settlements, refund programs and first-party terms. Every case links to its original record.

What these charts do not prove

How many consumers were harmed, present-day prevalence, intentional wrongdoing, or legal liability in pending cases. A settlement does not necessarily admit wrongdoing.
