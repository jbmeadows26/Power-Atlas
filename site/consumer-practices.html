WHO BENEFITS · WHO PAYS

Follow the product, the price and the decision.

Manufacturers choose pack sizes; retailers set shelf prices. Size cuts may reflect rising production costs, business strategy or other factors. These examples do not establish intent, profiteering or illegality.

What can consumers do?

Compare the unit price, check net weight or item count, and look at comparable alternatives rather than relying on package size.

FTC guidance ↗
Research methods, source links and important limits

GAO found that downsizing added less than 0.1 percentage point to the 34.5% rise in overall consumer prices during 2019–2024. Its category-specific figures above are percentage-point contributions to category CPI, not percentage changes in unit prices or measures of prevalence. Mouse Print documented the three package-size comparisons in October 2026; Power Atlas has not independently verified their historic prices, so associated unit-price changes are conditional illustrations, not confirmed company price increases.

What these charts do not prove
