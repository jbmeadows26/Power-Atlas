Three corrections the original graphic needed

Healthcare: The source spreadsheet calls $5.3 trillion U.S. healthcare spending in 2025. CMS reports approximately $5.3T in 2024 and projects around $5.7T in 2025. We label the 2025 number as a projection.

GDP: The source sheet places world GDP around $112T in 2025, closer to the 2024 estimate. World Bank 2025 world GDP is $118.35T. GDP is yearly output, not available cash.

Defense: Two independent agencies use different definitions. The IISS estimate is $2.63T in 2025, while SIPRI reports $2.887T. These are alternatives—not separate amounts to add together.
