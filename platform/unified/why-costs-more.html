What we verified: The BLS publishes CPI-U all-items annual averages of 258.811 (2020), 270.970 (2021), 292.655 (2022), 304.702 (2023), 313.689 (2024), and 321.943 (2025; based on available monthly observations, with October 2025 unavailable). We calculate the relative price level by dividing each by the 2020 value.

  • BLS annual and semiannual CPI index table — numeric basis for the interactive comparison.
  • BLS CPI questions and answers — methodology and what the CPI measures.
  • USDA food price outlook — food price categories and monitoring.
  • Census building permits — housing construction evidence.
  • FCC broadband consumer labels — plan-price disclosure.
  • FEC campaign finance data — contribution and committee records, not proof of causation.

Boundaries: CPI is a national consumer-price index, not a local advertised-rent dataset, an individual household budget, or a measure of wages or financial hardship. Price pathways are explanatory mechanisms and questions; they are not quantified causal attributions. No specific company or official is accused in this visual experience.

All content in this prototype is informational. Prices are not offered as a quote for any product, home, or service. Read the linked investigation pages for evidence linked to specific cases.
