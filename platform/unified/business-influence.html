3. What would prove an actual business advantage?

Power Atlas needs to connect five separate records, then compare with businesses that didn't donate.

01 · Who actually gave?

Match legal company and subsidiary IDs to permitted contribution records. Keep employee PAC money and executives' personal giving separate.

02 · What happened afterward?

Trace award IDs, agency decisions, bidding records, changes in regulation and subsidies by date—not just fiscal year.

03 · Compared with what?

Look at incumbent contracts, agency budgets, sector growth, non-donating competitors and profits. Include losses and null results.

Important legal distinction: Federal corporate contributions to candidates are generally prohibited, and special federal-contractor restrictions apply. Employee-funded PACs, independent political spending and state/local contributions can be governed differently. Similar names are not proof of a legal entity relationship.
