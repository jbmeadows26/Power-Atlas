Coverage and safeguards

Public links are not live ingestions. Research reports and allegations cannot establish legal violations. Archived services are not used as current data feeds. Stanford DIME includes redistribution restrictions for some third-party sourced itemized records; OpenSecrets bulk licensing and state-file privacy protections must be checked before publication. No private contact or address details are republished in this viewer.
