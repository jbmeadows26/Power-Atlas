Political influence · federal contracts · Sources reviewed Nov. 4, 2026

Public money. Campaign cash. What can we actually prove?

A defense contractor pursued Navy research funding while its leadership donated to politicians. Some campaign contributions were illegal. The broader allegation—that elected officials traded decisions for donations—is contested and was not proven in court.

The unanswered question

Were specific contracts traded for donations? Former CEO Martin Kao alleged this to the FBI, according to ProPublica. Collins and other elected officials or their representatives disputed wrongdoing. An investigation is not a conviction.
