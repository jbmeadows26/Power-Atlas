02 / The long view

How often do the parties vote far apart?

Each square represents one House or Senate congressional-period observation. The 119th Congress is still underway, so its observation is partial and is marked with an asterisk. Its color indicates the fraction of eligible roll calls meeting a strict opposition test: at least 90% of recorded votes within each party take opposite sides. This measures division in votes, not extremism or lawmakers' intentions.

How these squares are calculated

Equal squares count congressional periods. Scaled squares show the relative number of eligible roll calls—not population, intensity or social impact. The 119th Congress is incomplete. These historical aggregates cannot establish causes or individual intent.

What do these numbers mean?

Filed receipts: a bounded 2026 sample of committee receipt-side rows; not a nationwide census. Amendments are not fully reconciled.

Investigative leads: similar dates or amounts can justify further checking, not proof the same dollars moved.

Verified hidden origins: zero means none established in this dataset, not that dark money does not exist.

The figures have different units and denominators. Never add them or infer influence over a public official.
