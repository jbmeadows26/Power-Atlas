05 / What the data can actually say

Source first. Interpretation second.

Why this layout works

Inspired by Extremists on Both Sides, which uses one square per documented action, an equal-size versus magnitude toggle, filters, and a reader-controlled definition of a contested category. Power Atlas applies those mechanics only when the data support comparable groups and clear units.

Six evidence rules

  1. Documented acts are not proof of motive.
  2. Missing information is never treated as zero.
  3. Equal attention never implies equal conduct.
  4. Always show the denominator and time window.
  5. Law, proposals and research hypotheses stay separate.
  6. Claims remain inspectable at the primary source.
