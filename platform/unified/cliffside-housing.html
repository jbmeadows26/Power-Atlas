02 / What the rates mean

Same town. Different denominator.

▥General rate

Expressed per $100 of taxable assessed value. This helps calculate a standard property-tax levy, subject to parcel-specific rules and adjustments.

◎Effective statistical rate

Expressed as an aggregate indicator relative to market-value estimates. It is not a second rate to apply to the same assessed value.

Don't make this leap: A higher general rate does not prove every household's tax bill rose 2.90%. A lower effective rate does not mean anyone received a tax break. And neither rate tells us who owns a building, whether it has a PILOT, or who donated to a politician.

04 / The first dated decision

Here’s an agreement we can actually trace.

A 2019 borough resolution describes the Towne Centre redevelopment on Anderson Avenue, including a 2010 long-term tax exemption, two proposed condominium components, and actions authorized by the council in June 2019.

What did the borough require?

Resolution 2017-154 described $14,429,453 in outstanding redeveloper obligations if paid by July 21, 2017, or $14,529,453 if paid later by its stated deadline.

It also described a $12,005,000 original 2011 municipal bond and a proposed accelerated final repayment deadline of December 31, 2021, subject to executing amended agreements.

Not verified: whether either payment was made, whether the bond was redeemed, or whether the conditional agreements were executed. Dollar figures are not additive: the resolution covers different obligations and mechanisms.
