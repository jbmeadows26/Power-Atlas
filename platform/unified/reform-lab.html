A simple way to investigate

From a political dollar to a decision

Each step needs evidence. A connection on a chart is not automatically proof of influence.

01 · SOURCEWho provided money?

Named donor or disclosed organization; sometimes unknown.

02 · VEHICLEWhere did it go?

Committee, PAC, nonprofit, vendor or ad purchase.

03 · ACCESSWho met whom?

Reported lobbying, meetings, contracts or committee relationships.

04 · DECISIONWhat happened?

Legislative vote, rule, public contract or other official act.

Evidence rule: A disclosed payment can be documented. A suspicious timing pattern is only an investigative lead. Intent, influence, illegal coordination and hidden original donors are never inferred without additional evidence.
