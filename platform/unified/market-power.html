02 / The evidence test

Big is not the same as illegal.

Market concentration, market power, and proven anticompetitive conduct are distinct. Power Atlas will never turn a lawsuit allegation or a corporate ownership link into a finding of wrongdoing.

01 / Is there real competition?

Define the actual market.

Compare firms selling—or buying—the same service in the same geography. A national brand share cannot establish the choices available on your street.

02 / What happened?

Test an observed outcome.

Compare prices, wages, access, quality, entry and innovation. Costs, taxes, supply shocks and productivity can explain changes independently of monopoly power.

03 / What was proven?

Separate lawsuits from findings.

Label allegations, published studies, settled cases, court findings and regulatory requirements separately. Never invent a household monopoly markup.
