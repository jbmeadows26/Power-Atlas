Additional household fragility · November 13, 2026

Sports betting, prediction markets and smaller household buffers

Recent peer-reviewed studies and Federal Reserve research link expanded online betting access to lower savings and higher delinquency in studied groups. This is a potential amplifier of inflation, layoffs and expensive refinancing—not proof of systemic contagion.

Measured: The New York Fed estimated a 0.3 percentage-point rise in overall delinquency following mobile sports betting legalization. Original study ↗. Sportsbook handle and sports-related prediction-market activity may overlap and should never be summed as household losses. Explore the interactive household risk trail ↗.
Interacting risks · November 13, 2026

Energy, prices, employment, monetary policy, AI, tariffs and Treasury financing

The IMF identifies both an AI-linked correction and sovereign bond yield stress as potential market triggers. A confirmed energy-supply disruption, persistent inflation and expensive borrowing can compound household and business pressures without proving a future recession.

Evidence guardrails: November 13 Brent observations were below $100; September core PCE was 3.1%; October unemployment was 4.1%; October core PCE forecasts are not official results.
