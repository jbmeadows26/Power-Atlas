Privacy: your ZIP is saved only in this browser on your device so you can return without re-entering it. It is not placed in shared URLs or saved to a Power Atlas account. Postal and Census lookups still contact external providers.

How location data and rent sources work

When you submit, your five-digit ZIP is sent from your browser to Zippopotam.us for an approximate place name and Census Reporter for historic Census estimates. Those third parties have their own privacy practices. Bedroom rent comparisons download a state-level HUD-derived rent file from GitHub or Multifamily Today; the rent file request contains a state, not your individual ZIP. Power Atlas saves only your five ZIP digits locally in your browser until you choose Clear ZIP. Power Atlas does not store your ZIP on its servers or put it in a shared link. Power Atlas does not infer your politics from your ZIP. A ZIP is not a verified electoral, school, utility or city boundary.
