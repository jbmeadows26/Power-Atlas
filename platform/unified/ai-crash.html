Here's the uncomfortable question.

When one business funds another business that spends money back into the same ecosystem, the reported demand can be real while the financing network becomes more fragile.

Circular financing is a risk mechanism, not proof that sales are fake or that any individual participant has committed misconduct.

Fund a buildout today in exchange for expected returns tomorrow. Stress arrives if the future cash flows cannot support current valuations or debt service.

FICTIONAL SCENARIO

The correction stays in technology.

Investors lose money, but major customers keep spending and financing remains available. The technology develops even if valuations fall.

Limited spillover

Critical evidence: Sustained capital spending, credit-market stability and completed projects.

06 / AFTER THE FALL

Not everyone loses.
Not everyone is rescued.

A collapse in valuations can redistribute bargaining power. The winners, losers and public costs depend on the legal and financial response—not a preordained script.

EXPOSED TO LOSS
Investors, lenders & workers

Shareholders may absorb write-downs. Lenders can face defaults. Employees and contractors may face canceled projects. Losses are not equivalent across groups.

POTENTIAL OPPORTUNITY
Cash-rich buyers & customers

Strong balance sheets can acquire distressed assets. If AI compute becomes cheaper, businesses may benefit—even while investors lose money.

PUBLIC DECISIONS
Who gets protection?

Deposit guarantees, credit facilities, bankruptcy rules and targeted relief are different tools. Any future intervention requires its own evidence and accountability.

A government rescue is not assumed in any of these scenarios. For historical comparisons, investigate who received support, the ultimate taxpayer cost and recoveries—not just headline authorizations.
