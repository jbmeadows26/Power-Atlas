How to read a case

1 · Follow the money

Read the documented or alleged route from a donor or contractor through an intermediary to a public decision.

2 · Check each person's role

Distinguish people found guilty, those who pleaded guilty, those charged, and those cited in evidence.

3 · Inspect what remains unknown

Every case links to its government-source record and explains key missing evidence or limitations.
