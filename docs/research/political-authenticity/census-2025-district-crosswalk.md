# Census 2025 × 119th House district-key validation — 2026-10-30

The Census Bureau's [2025 ACS 1-year release](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-1year.2025.html) was published October 29, 2026. The [2020 apportionment](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2020/dec/2020-apportionment-data.html) assigns 435 voting seats. [119th district geography](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/rdo/congressional-districts.119th_Congress.html) is the correct January 2025 boundary vintage for ACS 2025; Alabama, Georgia, Louisiana, New York and North Carolina changed maps for the 2024 election.

The [crosswalk](../../../data/research/political-authenticity/house-district-crosswalk-119.json), [builder](../../../scripts/build_authenticity_district_crosswalk.py) and [tests](../../../tests/test_authenticity_district_crosswalk.py) validate all 435 voting seats against state apportionment and official Census FIPS. Against the October 29, 2026 roster, 433 seats are occupied; FL-20 and TX-23 are vacant. At-large districts use code 00; delegates are excluded. This fixes a prior gap where the age builder could accept a fictitious 433-district New Jersey fixture.

**Updated October 31, 2026:** The crosswalk's placeholder age fields remain null by design, but the [separate official 2025 B01001 district-age dataset](../../../data/research/political-authenticity/acs-2025-house-district-age.json) now includes observed age estimates for all **435 voting districts**, sourced from the [keyless Census FTP](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2025/table-based-SF/data/1YRData/acsdt1y2025-b01001.dat). The verified Census GEO_ID prefix is **5001900US**. [Ingestion methods and MOE caveats](acs-2025-district-age-ingestion.md). The age-band combined 90% MOEs are RSS approximations, not official aggregate MOEs. No permissioned ANES/CES district microdata, member wealth, occupations, or causal estimates are claimed. The 2024 national comparison remains a separately labelled cross-vintage baseline; sums over the 435 voting districts omit DC and territories.
