Bring one noncritical supported document, verify extracted information yourself, and inspect the original evidence behind any finding. The technical pilot is incomplete; participation is not an offer of guaranteed savings or validated forecasts.

## Consent-based pilot session

1. Explain local storage, encrypted backup, browser limitations and unsupported OCR.
2. Participant imports an actual document locally and approves facts. Researcher does not receive documents or profile data.
3. Participant checks a relevant finding, evidence date and limitations. Record a no-match outcome honestly if no applicable evidence exists.
4. Participant records accepted/rejected/deferred/acted decision and later a self-reported outcome in their own encrypted journal.
5. Participant may voluntarily provide an anonymized session summary: usefulness, decision changed or not, time to first value, false matches, missing inputs, usability, willingness to pay and measured costs if available. Consent and scope must precede any export or sharing.

No participant, saving, business outcome or willingness to pay may be invented. A meaningful decision change must link to the reviewed finding and dependency. Separate user report from independently verified outcome. Private records never train shared models automatically.

Operating-cost record: Actions execution minutes, Pages traffic, source failures, optional model download bytes and participant-observed inference duration. Do not equate free tier availability with unlimited capacity or future commercial hosting eligibility.
