Priority intelligence requirements

Each question must map to a decision, accountable source families, a freshness deadline and a defined evidence standard. These are proposed collection requirements, not confirmed finished assessments.

Collection disciplines and legal boundaries

The point is integration, not claiming access. Intelligence produced from declassified reporting is not evidence that this site itself collects those signals or human sources.

The six-stage intelligence cycle

Collection becomes actionable intelligence only after processing, competing-hypothesis analysis, dissemination and feedback. Each stage has a publication or review gate.

Prohibited publication practices

Live intake audit · measured, not assumed

Quality metrics are calculated from the current public intelligence intake, not from hypothetical scoring. Missing grading metadata is counted as missing—not silently upgraded to verified.

Reliability and credibility are separate

A reliable publisher can still issue a specific claim that is disputed, provisional or wrong. Sources are graded A–F; individual information is graded 1–6. Analyst confidence is a separate judgment.

Current gaps to close

System-wide PIR tagging, per-record source grades, independent corroboration groups, reviewer ownership and contradiction resolution must be built into intake and publication gates. The dashboard must never describe raw unreviewed intake as completed intelligence.

Official methodology: U.S. Intelligence Community · Intelligence Cycle · Live collection desk · Source library · Risk register. Historical findings, allegations, and analyst interpretations are distinct evidence categories.
