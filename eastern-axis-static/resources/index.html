Five different kinds of control

These power mechanisms must never be collapsed into a single unexplained “country control” score.

LEVER 01

Extraction rights

Who has legal access to the resource, and who operates the producing site?

LEVER 02

Processing capacity

Who turns a raw mineral or fuel into an industrially usable input?

LEVER 03

Ownership & finance

Who holds the equity, debt, concession or state-owned interest?

LEVER 04

Trade & transit

Which ports, pipelines, canals and borders connect supply to markets?

LEVER 05

Policy & coercion

Which export controls, sanctions, tariffs, contracts or armed conflicts alter access?

Research standards

Evidence before inference

The site separates observed values, documented relationships, a hypothesis, and missing data. No automatic guilt from geographic proximity.

Date every observation

A last-updated source is not the same as the date of the measurement. Every measurement needs its own reporting period and units.

Compare like with like

Oil transit is not oil production. Mineral mining and refining differ. Reserve volumes, supply security and ownership must not be merged.

Separate conflict from causality

An event near a mine does not prove an actor targeted that mine. Causal claims need corroborated documents or investigation.

Respect access & licenses

No scraping behind logins, bypassing keys or redistributing restricted data. Preserve license, attribution and rights in the source record.

No tactical targeting feed

Show strategic, public, aggregated resource exposure, not live sensitive facility vulnerabilities or tactical movement data.
