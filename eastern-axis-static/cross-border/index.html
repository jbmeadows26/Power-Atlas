04 / PROVENANCE & PUBLICATION

Observed ≠ inferred

Immutable source reference, retrieval time, SHA-256 of fetched bytes and an explicit record state. A soft-identity edge is a hypothesis, never a confirmed affiliation.

Access and licensing

Paid commercial providers, restricted registries and noncommercial-use datasets are flagged. No unlicensed copying or automated access to restricted services.

Release gate

Collection does not approve publication. Unverified records stay in the protected reviewer workflow; the public interface never auto-labels suspects.

Source statuses describe integration routes, not source availability guarantees. Public authorities are sources of record; third-party archives are identified as investigative leads. As of November 16, 2026.
