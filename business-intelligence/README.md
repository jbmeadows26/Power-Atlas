# Business Decision Intelligence — commercial pivot
## Mission
Know what's changing in the world, understand how it affects your business, and know what to consider doing.
Business Intelligence is the flagship commercial direction. Personal Intelligence remains the consumer arm. Eastern Axis and Power Atlas remain the shared public evidence and collection infrastructure. No existing platform or dataset is retired. The initial pilot audience is importers, distributors and midsize manufacturers. Target allocation: 60% business, 30% reliability/data quality, 10% personal maintenance.
## Pilot implementation
The isolated /business-intelligence/ workspace uses eight existing public repository feeds, with exact product group, country and corridor matching, explicitly entered monthly cost scenarios, date and source gates, and unresolved gaps. It never asserts that public proxy observations prove a company-specific shortage, cost increase or supplier disruption. No business profile is transmitted, persisted or sent to AI. The public model review workflow consumes only selected public input-price observations, invokes the shared Free Model Gateway in local-only mode, validates returned evidence IDs, and produces a review-only artifact. It never publishes model output into live decisions. Existing analyst automations remain unchanged until each passes a controlled migration.
## Commercial readiness gates
- Reliability: verify upstream APIs, freshness, retrieval-vs-observation dates, coverage, and production regression tests. Resolve unrelated failing checks rather than bypassing them.
- Rights: source-by-source review of commercial use, derivative works, attribution, redistribution, retention, API restrictions and third-party data terms. Public availability does not establish commercial rights. No paid access, licensing assurance, bulk redistributions or client report sales until approved.
- Security: tenant isolation, authenticated profiles and recovery, explicit supplier/document consent, deletion/export, least-privilege, separate private execution; never upload customer data to GitHub or hosted free models.
- Product: import actual purchase orders, vendor lists, SKUs/HS codes, locations, quantities, inventory coverage, alternate supplier quotes, lead times and operational constraints; prioritize supplier identity, tariff applicability, procurement and delivery decisions.
- Model: prove local inference, verify exact schema and known evidence IDs, benchmark against deterministic baseline; pin audited model/runtime dependencies and establish runner quotas before sustained production.
- Pilot: recruit 5–10 businesses, track decisions influenced, false positive rate, time to action, retention, data quality and willingness to pay. Prices $99/$299 are experiments, not activated billing. Personal $9 is also a hypothesis.
## Prioritized expansion, not active connections
1. Trade and tariffs: USITC HTS, Census international trade API, CBP and USTR notices; validate HS code, country of origin, effective dates, exceptions and duties. No automatic legal determination.
2. Supplier identity and exposure: official business registries, SEC EDGAR, OFAC and allied sanctions lists; disambiguation and ownership evidence before any counterparty flag.
3. Inventory and logistics: EIA, Census, IMF PortWatch, supplier/forwarder data; no route proxy treated as a shipment ETA.
4. Regulatory and cybersecurity: Federal Register, official regulator notices, CISA KEV; product/vendor identifiers and version applicability.
5. Private business imports: reuse document/OCR and transaction code with organization-specific review fields and no personal-to-business profile migration without user consent.
