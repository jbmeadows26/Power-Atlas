# Market power: evidence and integration protocol

**Status:** source-linked; 2026-11-10. These records are not a national monopoly index and should not be interpreted as proof of specific household overcharges.

## The story

A bill can be shaped by seller concentration (monopoly or oligopoly), buyer concentration (monopsony), vertical integration, control of access to a platform or distribution network, algorithmic coordination, regulatory barriers or natural-monopoly infrastructure. The beneficiaries and those bearing costs vary by market and time. Large-firm efficiency is a competing explanation; test it rather than dismiss it.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Court finding:** make the precise adjudicated finding, avoid expanding it to other products, markets or business conduct.
2. **Measured:** source-linked statistics or empirical findings. Include geography, market, year, numerator and denominator. Observational correlations are not causal estimates.
3. **Allegation:** complaint or open investigation; identify claimant, target, date and the procedural status. Settlements do not automatically prove liability.
4. **Context:** lawful regulated monopolies and other market structures are not by themselves antitrust violations.

## Measurement gates

- Define local geographic/product market before calculating market share, HHI and CR4. Corporate group and brand ownership must be canonicalized with time validity.
- Never sum the percentages in the gallery; they span different denominators. 51% in labor refers to a fraction of defined markets, not people. 
- Prices: estimate a market-power component only with a credible counterfactual and causal identification. Do not compute 'monopoly tax' by subtracting generic national CPI from a household bill.
- Labor: measure employer options by occupation and commuting zone; worker harm is not identical to seller-price inflation.
- Cross-category flow: firm/entity_id → ownership registry → market/region/time → conduct or structural measure → observed outcome → source and procedural status; treat campaign finance and lobbying as separate evidence tracks.
- Benefits: check passed-through productivity/scale gains, network quality and access before alleging net consumer harm.
- Compare alternatives: enforcement of existing law, structural merger remedies, interoperability, data portability, utility rate oversight, public procurement, worker mobility, small-business entry. Mark proposals as proposals.
- Keep official source links, publication date, update date, legal status, claim-versus-finding distinction, and flags for stale observations.

## Scope gaps before any causal public claim

No verified county/ZIP-specific provider shares; no estimated household 'monopoly surcharge'; no universal national profit attributable to concentration; no causal attribution of market prices to political donations; and no automatic validation of allegations. These are explicit unavailable measures, not zero values.
