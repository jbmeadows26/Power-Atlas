# Causal identification safeguards

The 407 trust numbers are country-wave-institution cells, not 407 independent longitudinal observations. Do not use them to fit Arellano-Bond. The OECD Trust Survey was fielded in 2021, 2023 and 2025, with varying country availability. Do not interpolate missing outcomes or substitute Doing Business or GovTech digital maturity for experienced administrative burden. A 2×2 randomized study is a proposed design, not a completed experiment.
