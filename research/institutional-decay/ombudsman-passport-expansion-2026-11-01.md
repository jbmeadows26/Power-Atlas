# Institutional decay — Ireland, New Zealand and US oversight/service expansion (1 November 2026)

**Scientific status:** source-attributed descriptive evidence only; no causal identification, no individual misconduct presumption, no country-level institutional-decay score. This file documents the additive dataset [ombudsman-passport-evidence-2026-11-01.json](../../data/institutional-decay/ombudsman-passport-evidence-2026-11-01.json), with **8 official source records, 19 observations, and 8 dated timeline events**. The events are dated by source publication or official investigation notice, not by assumed underlying conduct dates.

## Verified additions

1. **Ireland — both complaint volume and accountability outcomes.** The Ombudsman reported 4,778 complaints received in 2024 and 5,297 in 2025; 4,996 complaints investigated in 2025 and 1,793 complaints about local authorities in 2025. Its 2024 report says 3,722 complaints were resolved within three months and 4,329 within six months (of 4,673 dealt with); 27% of eligible investigated cases were fully or partly upheld. That **27% denominator excludes withdrawn/discontinued matters** and is not a share of complaints received. The reported increase in complaints is not, by itself, a measure of increasing misconduct or declining trust. Sources: [2025 report release](https://www.ombudsman.ie/en/news/9d6a5-ombudsman-receives-highest-number-of-public-service-complaints-in-over-30-years/) and [2024 annual report](https://assets.ombudsman.ie/media/285560/f4acdeaa-08f1-49d4-992a-63d70ba2d099.pdf).
2. **Ireland — documented administrative correction, not personal guilt.** Two anonymized Ombudsman case studies published 11 July 2024 describe (a) the Department of Social Protection dropping a €26,000 Pandemic Unemployment Payment recovery after reconsidering entitlement and (b) a Carer's Allowance appeal revised after a medical assessment had not been considered, with nine months' arrears of €6,078. These are documented agency case resolutions, **not criminal convictions or representative error rates**. The source publication date is known; underlying decision/resolution dates are not established. Sources: [PUP case](https://ombudsman.ie/en/collection/4ee69-department-of-social-protection-wrongly-sought-to-recover-26000-pandemic-social-welfare-payment/) and [Carer's Allowance case](https://ombudsman.ie/en/collection/b42a5-man-was-wrongly-refused-carers-allowance-when-medical-report-was-missed-by-appeals-office/).
3. **New Zealand — oversight throughput, with competing interpretations.** For FY2024/25 the Ombudsman reported 8,163 completed official-information/Ombudsmen Act complaints, 5,269 Ombudsmen Act complaints received, 2,554 official-information complaints received, 278 completed protected disclosures/enquiries, and a reported 104% clearance rate. Clearing more cases than received can reduce backlogs; higher incoming complaints may reflect public awareness, easier access or service problems. The release does not establish their relative causes. [Official 21 October 2025 release](https://www.ombudsman.parliament.nz/news/another-record-year-office-ombudsman).
4. **New Zealand — allegations remain allegations.** The Chief Ombudsman announced a formal investigation on **25 September 2026** into allegations of attempts to circumvent the Official Information Act at the Ministry of Education. On **2 October 2026**, the Ombudsman formally notified the Ministry of an investigation into practices within its Curriculum Centre, including recordkeeping and compliance with the OIA and Public Records Act. Neither cited statement reports a completed finding or sanction. Sources: [announcement](https://www.ombudsman.parliament.nz/news/chief-ombudsman-conduct-investigation-oia-concerns-ministry-education) and [formal notice](https://www.ombudsman.parliament.nz/news/chief-ombudsman-formally-notifies-ministry-education-investigation).
5. **United States — explicit counterexample to universal deterioration.** GAO-25-107409 reports that National Passport Information Center average caller waits were **approximately 45 minutes in June 2023** but **less than one minute in June 2024**. Staffing was approximately 500 agents in FY2020 and 1,600 in FY2024, and approximately 4.6 million calls were answered in FY2024. The <1 minute figure is stored as a strict upper bound, not exactly one minute. GAO describes staffing, technology and other changes; these descriptive comparisons **do not isolate any one cause**. [GAO report published 8 July 2025](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107409/index.html).

## Source reuse and access checks

- **Ireland:** [Office of the Ombudsman PSI policy](https://www.ombudsman.ie/en/collection/24f96-re-use-of-public-sector-information-psi/) permits free reuse with attribution, accuracy and non-misleading-use conditions. Short adapted numerical and case-outcome descriptions only; no names of anonymized complainants or case narrative copied.
- **United States:** GAO report expressly states it is US federal-government work, not subject to US copyright protection, with a warning about third-party materials. No images, graphs or third-party works copied.
- **New Zealand:** Exact copyright/reuse terms applicable to the specific Ombudsman news pages could **not be independently confirmed**. Accordingly only short factual counts and dated public procedural facts were transcribed; no copyrighted expression, graphics, bulk extracts or personal data. This is a **licensing metadata gap** before any redistribution of richer NZ source material.
- **Access limitations:** Some Irish official HTML pages returned HTTP 403 to the browsing reader; indexed official excerpts and the official annual-report PDF provided a legitimate read-only alternative. No access controls, robots rules or rate limits were bypassed. This limits independent full-page HTML inspection; the source URLs remain retained for audit.

## Validation and compatibility

The dataset uses the existing `power-atlas.institutional-decay.accountability-service-cases.v1` schema. Unique IDs, HTTPS source references, source-publication dates, event ordering, evidence classes, numerical units, known denominators and strict upper-bound precision are checked in [stdlib regression tests](../../tests/test_institutional_decay_ombudsman_passport.py). The [path-scoped GitHub Actions workflow](../../.github/workflows/validate-institutional-decay-ombudsman-passport.yml) is intended to run on changes to these assets. A committed workflow is **not** evidence that a run succeeded; confirm Actions separately.

**No production integration:** No changes to `data/app-feed.json`, the redistricting API/feed, production UX, or a composite institutional score. These cross-country agency/case observations cannot be pooled as equivalent population denominators. No individual trust microdata, representative experiment or identified causal feedback panel was added.

**Outstanding scientific blockers:** Representative randomized study with consent/recruitment; comparable microdata and valid cross-country denominators; robust temporal and causal identification. The current source set supports transparent case comparisons and falsification checks, not H3/H6 causal claims.
