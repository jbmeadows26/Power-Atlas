## Fiscal-election extension and competing explanations

[USAspending's public API](https://api.usaspending.gov/) is a legitimate no-fee source for U.S. federal awards/account-level data. It is **not a substitute** for the existing Census state direct capital-outlay panel; award announcements, obligations, cash disbursements and capital assets are different measures. Canadian Budget 2025 provides *forecast* funding and a federal/operating-capital classification, not comparable observed U.S. state finance. No cross-national coefficient is computed.

Retain the [existing observed U.S. state election association assessment](observed-election-associations-2012-2024.md): 650 real state-year observations and six exploratory regression specifications, with noncausal interpretations, provisional fiscal clocks and a 2022 Census classification break. Competing mechanisms to evaluate include legislative control, coalition compromise, implementation capacity, tax-base changes, inflation, exogenous shocks, court action, election timing endogeneity, pre-budget economic conditions, and later policy reversals. A positive or negative regression association does not establish political motive.

## Required next evidence gates

1. **Denominator:** freeze party/candidate scope, cutoffs and document versions; enumerate *all* candidate sentences including negative/exclusion decisions, deduplicate repeated and compound promises, then independently adjudicate eligibility. Do not add 12 to an unknown national denominator.
2. **Actions:** for every pledge build a dated event chain of bill, vote, signed law, regulations, commencement, appropriation, obligation, payment and delivered result. Null outcome is not a failure. Terms still running are censored.
3. **Review:** obtain two genuinely independent human coders, adjudication disagreements, measure inter-rater reliability and record counterevidence. Machine-generated checks are not independent human review.
4. **Fiscal:** verify source coverage, calendar and accounting crosswalk, country-specific revisions, appropriations versus actuals, currency/price adjustments and confidence intervals before estimation.
5. **Rights:** maintain links, locators and short paraphrases. Party archives and PDFs are not assumed licensed for bulk text redistribution. Confirm access terms before further ingestion.
