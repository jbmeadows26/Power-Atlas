# Financial flows involving Donald Trump and related entities — November 10, 2026

## Scope and publication criteria
This is a **nonpartisan, source-linked catalogue** of disclosed income and financial interests, corporate transactions, foreign-government asset transfers, settlements designated for library entities, spouse-reported business income, and announced access-related token events. It is not a total financial-benefit claim, allegation score, finding of illegality, or predictive measure of consumer harm. Sources, recipients, metric units, event dates and open uncertainties must be explicit in the frontend.

## Principal records

**Primary document:** Donald J. Trump, 2025 Annual Executive Branch Public Financial Disclosure Report, received by OGE June 29 and certified June 30, 2026: https://extapps2.oge.gov/201/Presiden.nsf/PAS+Index/69AEAA9D7455ACD585258E27002DDEE1/$FILE/Donald-J-Trump-2026-278ANNUAL.pdf . Verified original pages: 1 (late-filing-fee note), 6 (spouse income), 848 (CIC Digital royalty), 854 (stablecoin), 855 (WLF token and equity proceeds). **Raw pages are PDF viewer page numbers, not necessarily the numbered schedule pages.**

- **CIC Digital LLC, 2025 royalties:** $635,068,835 — 2026 OGE report, Schedule 1 Part 2, line 21.6. Connected to meme-coin and NFT licensing; entity owned by the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust. **Not** a per-purchaser receipt or audited net profit.
- **DT Marks Defi / World Liberty:** $236,250,000 net proceeds on token sale line 124.3, $65,625,000 in equity proceeds on line 124.2. Nine additional wallet lines separately total $292,388,944 per independent line review, which may **duplicate** the $236,250,000. Do not sum without a filing-level reconciliation. https://withreceipts.fyi/donald-trump/cryptocurrency-earnings/
- **Stablecoin Holdco / DT Marks SC:** $196,875,000 net proceeds from capital/unit sales, Schedule 1 Part 2 line 114 (PDF viewer page 854). This is not the same as the sovereign-linked $2 billion MGX transaction.
- **Foreign business receipts:** CREW reported more than $117 million from overseas business interests for 2025 based on OGE filings. Approx. $38 million Gulf project licensing is a subset, **not** another independent total. The entities include private developers; do not treat every foreign licensee as a foreign government.
- **MGX/Binance USD1:** $2 billion MGX investment in Binance was announced as using World Liberty's USD1 stablecoin. This is transaction **principal** between an Abu Dhabi-backed investor and Binance, **not $2B paid to the President**. Reuters May 1, 2025.
- **Aqua 1 Foundation:** $100 million token purchase announcement; Reuters could not independently verify key aspects of the self-described UAE entity, its personnel or funding source. Mark ownership and origin **unverified**.
- **ALT5 Sigma:** SEC 2025 filing describes approx $1.5B financing and WLFI token treasury strategy. Corporate financing may overlap token sales and is not income to Trump.
- **Qatar Boeing 747:** Pentagon accepted aircraft from Qatar in May 2025 for possible use as a U.S. presidential aircraft. Source: Associated Press. White House told ABC in August 2026 no final future disposition had been made. Explicitly distinguish transfer **to Department of Defense** from personal receipt or a completed library transfer. Foreign Emoluments Clause and Foreign Gifts and Decorations Act questions are legal issues, not automatically adjudicated violations.
- **ABC, Meta and Paramount settlements:** reported library-destination allocations of $15M, $22M and $16M respectively. X-related funds of $8M discussed by a July 2026 congressional inquiry had unclear final destination. Settlement terms, funds and nonprofit tax filings are **not** equivalent to a transfer into a personal checking account. The congressional inquiry is a request for information, not a proved diversion.
- **Spouse disclosure:** 2025 financial disclosure Part 5 contains $10.71M film-related net proceeds to MKT World LLC. This remains spouse-related and not Trump's personal income.
- **$TRUMP dinners:** May 2025 event for holders and a planned November 22, 2026 event; eligibility based on token holdings rather than a known seat price. Offshore-exchange-linked wallets do not prove nationality of beneficial owners. White House described 2025 dinner as private and no promise of government action.
- **Late fees:** OGE's June 2026 cover note says late-filing fees were paid for 278-T transactions not previously reported, which is a disclosure-compliance record, not a financial benefit received.

## Dataset / graphical implementation
- `data/public-integrity/official-financial-flows-v1.json` is the reusable record register.
- `platform/unified/official-financial-flows-v1.json` is a bit-for-bit JSON mirror for the standalone live page.
- `platform/unified/financial-flows.html` is the newsroom-style explorer with a 4-line income chart, recipient changeable path, 20 record cards, categorical filters, expandable caveats and clickable first-party or traceable evidence.
- `platform/unified/public-trading.html` retains the separate Kadoa/OGE monthly securities disclosure comparison. Stock trade volume is not summed into revenues, gifts, corporate deals, fees or donations.
- `platform/unified/index.html`, `my-life.html`, `donor-intelligence.html`, `business-influence.html` and `corporate-ownership.html` link to the new investigation.
- The `2025` income period is not equivalent to the inauguration-onwards period; some announced or settled transactions span 2024–2026.
- Do not display unsupported tax effects, inflation costs or illegal-conduct flags.
